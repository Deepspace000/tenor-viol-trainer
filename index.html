Tuned G C F A D G (low to high): fourths with a major third between strings 3 and 4 (F–A). It sits a fourth above the bass viol (D G C E A D). The tenor is non-transposing. Typically 7 gut frets, tied on and movable; they are a semitone apart, so the names here are the equal-tempered ones (real viols often use meantone).

Clefs: the tenor viol usually reads alto clef (C clef on the middle line, which is middle C). Some editions use tenor clef (C on the 4th line) or octave treble, and high parts can be in treble at pitch. Landmarks: middle line = C4; top line = G4 (open 1st string); bottom line = F3 (open 4th string).

Sources: VdGSA viol help sheet, Saraband: Viola & Tenor Viol, Writing for viol and voices.
